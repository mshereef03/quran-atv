## Description

A premium, native Quran experience for Apple TV — clean, simple, and built with care for the big screen.

The Noble Quran turns your living room into a place for reflection. Arabic text highlights in sync as each ayah is recited, presented with the kind of polish and simplicity you'd expect from a first-party Apple TV app.

FOLLOW ALONG
• Live Arabic text highlighting, synced precisely to the recitation
• Optional English translation, shown alongside the Arabic
• Adjustable Arabic typeface and text size, so it's comfortable to read from across the room

BROWSE YOUR WAY
• Browse all 114 surahs, or by juz'
• Filter by Meccan or Medinan revelation
• Search by name
• Bookmark favorites and resume exactly where you left off

MULTIPLE RECITERS
Choose from reciters including Mishari Rashid al-'Afasy, Abdul Basit Abdul Samad, Mahmoud Khalil Al-Husary, Abdur-Rahman as-Sudais, and more — switch reciters anytime, even mid-surah.

BUILT FOR THE SIRI REMOTE
Every screen is designed around focus-driven navigation and your Apple TV remote — no app-within-an-app, no touchscreen patterns awkwardly ported to the big screen.

Free. No account required. No ads.

Quran text, translations, and recitation audio are provided by Quran Foundation.
